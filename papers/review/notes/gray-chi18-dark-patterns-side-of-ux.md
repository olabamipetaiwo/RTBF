# Gray, Kou, Battles, Hoggatt, Toombs. The Dark (Patterns) Side of UX Design

ACM CHI 2018 (April 21-26, 2018, Montreal), Paper 534, 14 pages (the PDF is marked "Paper 534"; page range to take from the publisher record), DOI 10.1145/3173574.3174108 as in the manifest. File: `4_users/gray-chi18-dark-patterns-side-of-ux.pdf` (ACM PDF, downloaded by the user through the UCF library). Read the abstract, introduction, the corpus and analysis method, the five strategy categories and the "roach motel" passage. I did not read the discussion of designer ethics in detail.

## Question
What ethical concerns do UX practitioners raise when they name "dark patterns", and how can the patterns be organised (Abstract)?

## Method
- Assembled a corpus of 118 practitioner-identified examples (45 from social media, 40 from practitioner blogs, 19 from news outlets, 10 from the authors' own use of products, 4 from darkpatterns.org), then used constant comparative analysis and document analysis, starting from Brignull's taxonomy (Section 3).

## Findings
- Five strategy categories of dark patterns: nagging, obstruction, sneaking, interface interference and forced action, with Brignull's categories nested inside them (Section 4).
- The "roach motel", where signing up is easy and cancelling or closing an account is difficult or impossible, belongs to obstruction. The paper's example is a service that required a phone call to cancel (Section 4).
- The practitioners' concerns were wider than the label, and the authors say designers can easily become complicit in manipulative design (Abstract).

## Limits the authors state
The corpus consists of examples that practitioners chose to report, so it is not a count of how common each pattern is, and the categories come from the authors' interpretation.

## How it differs from ours
- A taxonomy from practitioner examples, with no measurement of prevalence and nothing about whether deletion takes effect. The roach-motel and obstruction categories name the difficulty of leaving or deleting, which the audit papers (Rupp, Yan, Habib 2020) measure.

## Use in our paper
- Cite for the name and definition of the roach motel (leaving is harder than joining) if we need to describe why deletion paths are hard to find. Peripheral. It can be dropped without loss; if cited, pair it with Mathur et al. (2019), which measured prevalence.
